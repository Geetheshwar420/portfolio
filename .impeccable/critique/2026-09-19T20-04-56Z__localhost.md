### Design Specificity Verdict

However, relying entirely on Next.js default `Geist` and decorative grid lines pushes it slightly back toward "category-interchangeable AI generated" territory. 

**Deterministic scan**: The CLI detector found 3 issues (0 errors, 2 warnings, 1 advisory). It correctly identified the use of an overused font (`Geist`), an unoptimized layout animation (animating `width` instead of `transform`), and the use of the generic decorative `codex-grid-background`.

### Overall Impression
A highly professional, aesthetically pleasing portfolio. The editorial typography works beautifully, but small polish details (harsh gradients, generic fonts, contrast) are holding it back from being truly *impeccable*.

### What's Working
- **Typography Contrast**: The mix of display fonts with crisp sans-serif/monospace tags is elegant.
- **Section Pacing**: The flow from Hero to Projects to Skills to Contact feels intentional and well-structured.
- **Minimalist Restraint**: Removing the redundant nav links tightened up the header beautifully.

### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Form Placeholder Contrast**
  - **Why it matters**: Low contrast on the "Your name" and email inputs makes the form inaccessible and hard to use in dark mode.
  - **Fix**: Brighten placeholder colors (`::placeholder` pseudo-class) to meet WCAG AA standards.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

- **[P2] Heavy Header Vignette**
  - **Why it matters**: The sticky header gradient casts a harsh, heavy shadow that clips content awkwardly when scrolling.
  - **Fix**: Soften the `backdrop-filter` blur and reduce the opacity of the gradient overlay.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

- **[P3] Layout Animation Thrashing**
  - **Why it matters**: Animating `width` (as flagged by the detector) causes browser layout thrashing and jank.
  - **Fix**: Refactor animations to use `transform: scaleX()` or `opacity`.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable optimize`

- **[P3] Generic "Codex" Visuals**
  - **Why it matters**: The `Geist` font and decorative grid lines scream "default Next.js template."
  - **Fix**: Swap to a more distinct geometric sans or adjust the grid line opacity/pattern to feel more authored.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`
